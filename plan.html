Plan your trip to Galway and Connemara

Whether you are visiting for a weekend or a longer stay, here are a few things to think about before you travel.

Getting here

Galway can be reached by bus or train from Dublin. If you are visiting Connemara, check local bus timetables or consider hiring a car.

Where to stay

Galway City is a good base for restaurants and nightlife. Clifden and Letterfrack are useful locations for exploring Connemara.

What to pack

The weather can change quickly. Bring a raincoat, comfortable walking shoes and a few warm layers, especially for coastal walks.
